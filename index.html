Frequently Asked Questions

What is ETHORISE?

ETHORISE is an independent affiliate product discovery platform where visitors can explore product information and follow links to retailers.

Does ETHORISE sell products directly?

No. Purchases are completed on third-party retailer websites, such as Amazon or AliExpress.

How do affiliate links work?

Some links may earn ETHORISE a commission when an eligible purchase is completed.

How can I check product prices?

Visit the retailer to check current prices, product details and availability.

Can I buy multiple products together?

You can organize multiple products in your ETHORISE Shopping List. Each product's purchase link opens its retailer. Quantities are not automatically transferred to retailer checkout.

Who handles shipping and returns?

The retailer where you place your order handles applicable shipping, returns and refunds.
