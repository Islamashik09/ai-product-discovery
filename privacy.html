Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 12, 2026

Welcome to ETHORISE.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when you visit our website, browse products, save favorites, or follow links to third-party retailers.

ETHORISE currently operates as a product discovery and affiliate marketing website. We do not directly process product purchases or collect payment card details.

1. Information We Collect

Our current website does not require visitors to create an account.

Search terms and product filtering are currently processed within your browser.

If you contact us through a communication channel we provide, we may receive information you voluntarily share, such as your name, email address, and message.

2. Browser Local Storage

ETHORISE uses browser local storage to remember certain preferences.

  • Products saved to your wishlist.
  • Products selected for comparison.

These preferences are stored in your browser.

You can remove this information by clearing the website's local storage through your browser settings.

3. Cookies and Third-Party Services

Our website may load resources from third-party services, including Google Fonts and externally hosted images.

These providers may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details when your browser requests their resources.

Our website is hosted using GitHub Pages. Hosting providers may process technical information necessary to deliver the website and maintain security.

4. Affiliate Links

ETHORISE participates in affiliate marketing programs.

When you click an affiliate link, you may be redirected to a third-party retailer such as Amazon or AliExpress.

Affiliate networks and retailers may use cookies or other tracking technologies to attribute eligible purchases.

Their use of information is governed by their own privacy policies.

For more information, please read our Affiliate Disclosure .

5. Amazon Associates

ETHORISE participates in the Amazon Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program that allows eligible websites to earn advertising fees by linking to Amazon.

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

You can learn more about Amazon's privacy practices by visiting Amazon's official Privacy Notice.

6. How Information May Be Used

Information processed through the website may be used to:

  • Display relevant product information.
  • Support product search and filtering.
  • Remember wishlist and comparison selections within your browser.
  • Respond to inquiries you voluntarily submit.
  • Maintain website functionality and security.

7. Third-Party Websites

ETHORISE contains links to websites operated by third parties.

We do not control their privacy practices, payment systems, shipping policies, or customer service procedures.

Before making a purchase, please review the retailer's applicable policies.

8. Data Security

We aim to minimize unnecessary collection of personal information.

However, no website or online communication method can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

ETHORISE is intended for a general shopping audience and is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not intentionally request personal information from children through our current website.

10. International Visitors

ETHORISE may be accessed by visitors from different countries.

Third-party hosting providers, affiliate networks, and retailers may process information in different jurisdictions.

11. Your Privacy Choices

You may manage browser cookies, clear local storage, and adjust browser privacy settings.

Where applicable law provides additional privacy rights, you may contact us to make an appropriate request.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when website functionality, services, or legal requirements change.

Updated information will be published on this page with a revised date.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please visit our Contact Page .
